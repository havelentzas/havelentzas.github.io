Day5
Friday, June 19

Picos de Europa — Ruta del Cares

One of Spain's most spectacular mountain hikes
⌄
Accommodation — Night 5
Same as Night 4 — Arenas de Cabrales
€60.00
Ruta del Cares
  • Trail: Poncebos → Caín through the Cares Gorge — 12 km one way
  • Carved into vertical limestone cliffs; the gorge drops 1,000m below the path
  • Allow 3–4h one way. Return by bus from Caín or walk back (~7–8h total)
  • Start early (8am) to beat the heat and crowds
  • Alternative: Naranjo de Bulnes (Picu Urriellu) viewpoint hike from Sotres
  • Post-hike: river swim at Poncebos or cold beer in the village square
