After 6 months of planning, hundreds of emails, and countless hours of work, I am incredibly proud to share that[**European Horizons Leiden University**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/european-horizons-leiden-university/) hosted the 2026 Youth Security Conference on Saturday. It was our biggest conference yet with 3 panels of expert speakers, 3 workshops, networking, and more. The day was kicked off by a keynote speech from Lt. Gen. (ret.) [**Rob Bertholee**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robbertholee/), speaking from his decades of command in the Royal Netherlands Army, as well as his later position as the head of the [**Algemene Inlichtingen- en Veiligheidsdienst - AIVD**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/algemene-inlichtingen--en-veiligheidsdienst---aivd/) (Dutch Intelligence Service). His insights into the military and intelligence worlds were unparalleled, offering students a learning experience that could not be found in the classroom, something that would prove to be a trend throughout the conference.

For Panel 1: Transnational Military Cooperation, We were joined by former Secretary General of the European Parliament[**Klaus Welle**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/klaus-welle-37561938/), Lt. Col. [**Jens Kimmel**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jens-kimmel-b414059b/) from the NATO Joint Support and Enabling Command in Ulm Germany, Dr. [**Ernst Dijxhoorn**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ernstdijxhoorn/) of [**Leiden University**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/leiden-university/)'s Institute of Security and Global Affairs, and [**Davis Ellison**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/davis-h-ellison/) of [**HCSS - The Hague Centre for Strategic Studies**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-hague-centre-for-strategic-studies/). These experts from across the government, military, and academia gave students varied perspectives on questions like the future of NATO, EU security strategy, and bilateral military cooperation.

Panel 2: The Shifting Role of Technology in Defense, Government, and Industry. Panelists discussed technologies like artificial intelligence, drones, and the ever growing importance of cybersecurity. Founder and Managing Director of[**MERCURY AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS B.V.**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mercury-unmanned-aerial-solutions/) [**Adriaan Wiese**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hawiese/) brought the private sector perspective. Royal Netherlands Army Col. [**Hans van Dalen**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hans-van-dalen-8302ab19/) gave the military perspective. Dr. [**Lukas Milevski**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lukas-milevski-b7348830/) of the Leiden University Institute of History brought his expertise in military strategy to talk about how past developments can prepare us for the future of warfare. 

Panel 3: Security at Europe's Periphery came at a crucial time with wars in Ukraine and the Near East, the military perspective of Rear Admiral [**Jeanette Morang**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeanette-morang-83344474/), the legal perspective from [**Georgia Canură**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/georgiacanura/) of the [**Center for International Legal Cooperation**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/center-for-international-legal-cooperation/), and the public private partnership perspective of [**Lotte Harbers**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lotte-harbers-4525694/), gave students a well rounded image of how Europe can tackle hybrid security threats on every front. 

As Deputy Secretary-General of the conference I am incredibly proud of what the team achieved. Alongside our Secretary-General [**Sander Versteijnen**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sander-versteijnen-66461726b/) I oversaw talented and dedicated fellow students as we contacted potential speakers & sponsors, promoted the event to our peers, and organized the logistics for the day. The conference was truly a testament to European Horizons' by students for students ethos, one which resonates deeply with me.

We are incredibly grateful to our speakers who made such an ambitious event possible and who gave their time for the next generation who dreams of working in fields like theirs.
