🏀 Two games, two of Europe's greatest cities 🇩🇪🇬🇧

I was incredibly lucky to help bring the [National Basketball Association (NBA)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/national-basketball-association/) experience to everyday fans and VIPs alike this past week with the NBA Berlin and London games.

Berlin showed me how the pros do live production & entertainment. In my first production meeting I heard an insightful (if not funny) quote "They are playing a basketball game around our show". On game days it truly felt like that and I think there is no better way to capture the showmanship that goes into live sports. The uncertainty of when a timeout would come necessitated dynamism and preparedness at all times that only rehearsal and real team synergy could bring.

London was a crash course in team coordination. Tight schedules, hectic travel, and long days meant that working together was crucial. I was fortunate to work with the experienced and professional [Joshua Lowes](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joshua-lowes-2464531b3/), [DJ Cadz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dj-cadz-bb328b353/), [Alanna Wells](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alanna-jay-wells/), and [Tom Smith](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tomjamessmith/), who were incredibly kind to assist me in the logistics behind the fan favorite free stuff moments. I am eternally thankful to [Chloe Marshall-Walker](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chloe-marshall-walker-73166310b/) for allowing me to take the lead managing that. It was the type of responsibility that pushed me just far enough out of my comfort zone to learn under pressure, while still delivering on my obligations.

London was also my first time contributing to the VIP experience. London, even more than the NBA's other global games, was packed with stars. From the red carpet to court side, I assisted Marion Gomez, Grace Ishiki, and [Eugenia Montoya Kent](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eugenia-montoya-kent/), in assuring London's VIPs had a smooth and enjoyable experience with ticketing, security, and seating.

Across both games the underlying lesson I learned was how to bring the fan experience across borders and across cultures, in the case of Berlin, for the very first time. The guidance and insight from both NBA staff/contractors and our local colleagues gave me knowledge and experience that simply can't be found in a classroom.
