I joined [**European Horizons Leiden University**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/european-horizons-leiden-university/) in October of 2024 as Public Relations Media Coordinator. in the the 19 months since then I became the Head of Public Relations and later also Deputy-Secretary General of our recent conference. But more importantly I found a genuine love and care for the organization's objectives: delivering education, publishing, and connectivity opportunities to students.

I never truly understood what it meant to be "mission driven" before European Horizons. Seeing the purpose and product of your work is a feeling like no other. Being only a small part of the team that has brought truly unique opportunities to[**Leiden University**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/leiden-university/) students has been incredibly fulfilling, and that makes me beyond excited to share that my journey with European Horizons does not end here.

As we transition from Board II to Board III I will be stepping into a new role as the Head of Affairs. In this position I will be leveraging my grasp on European Horizons as an organization to form and manage relations with external partners as well as our members.
